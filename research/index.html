Research Summary: DAP Global Inc. and the Website Manager Role

What the job actually owns, what the company looks like, what customers say, and what dap.com does well and badly today. Researched November 6, 2026 from primary sources: the Oracle posting, dap.com itself, retailer review pages, and competitor sites.

The role

TitleWebsite Manager, DAP Global Inc.
LocationBaltimore, MD, hybrid, full-time
Pay$95,000 to $110,000 per year
PostingOracle requisition 16401, published October 30, 2026
Experience5 to 7 years; Bachelor's in Marketing, Communications, Digital, or Business, or equivalent

The role is the marketing owner of dap.com and other brand sites: website priorities and roadmap, CMS publishing, audience journeys, performance and KPIs, localization, accessibility and governance, PIM/DAM product content, and agency and vendor management including budget.

Preferred qualifications named in the posting

  • Sitecore or a comparable enterprise CMS
  • CPG or another product-led consumer business
  • Working with web agencies and vendors, including scopes, estimates, invoices, and budget tracking
  • Multilingual or localized web experiences
  • Web accessibility and privacy/compliance familiarity
  • SEO, AEO, and GEO working knowledge
  • PIM/DAM-supported product content and digital assets
  • Website integrations and external services, owned from the business side
  • Basic HTML/CSS for troubleshooting
  • Demonstrated examples of measurable website improvement

The company

  • DAP Products Inc., maker of latex caulks, silicone sealants, adhesives, insulating foams, and patch and repair products, including Alex, Kwik Seal, Dynaflex, DryDex, Weldwood, Touch 'n Foam, RapidFuse, and Plastic Wood.
  • An operating company of RPM International Inc. (NYSE: RPM). Founded 1865 in Dayton, Ohio as a sealing wax maker; headquartered in Baltimore since 1998 at 2400 Boston Street, with a plant at 4630 North Point Blvd.
  • Claims the number one caulk brand position in America; roughly 775+ associates, 8 US and Canada facilities, products in about 60,000 retail outlets.
  • Benefits in the posting include a 401(k), an employer-funded pension, and tuition reimbursement.

Google business reviews for the Baltimore listings are thin and are not the story: the headquarters listing returned no rating, and the plant shows 3.5 stars on just 2 reviews. BBB shows 6 complaints in 3 years. The real evidence is Home Depot product reviews, checked November 2026:

Recurring low-rating themes

  • Shrinking and cracking, the dominant complaint across the Alex family.
  • Never curing or washing away, concentrated in Kwik Seal Plus, the weakest flagship at 37% recommend.
  • Mold despite mold-resistance claims, and color mismatch, including clear product that applies white and takes 7 to 14 days to turn clear.
  • Service friction: DAP's templated retailer replies cite label exclusions and point to a phone number, which reviewers read as blame. The warranty itself is mail-in: used container plus receipt to Baltimore for replacement or refund only.
  • Packaging: no way to reseal, product dried out in the tube.

One honest negative: no reviews found complained about dap.com or about finding SDS/TDS documents. The website findings below come from auditing the site directly, not from reviews.

Employee reviews (Glassdoor) sit at 4.0 stars with 79% recommend; one 2020 IT analyst review said technology leadership lacks vision. No marketing or digital team reviews were found.

Audit of dap.com

  • Stack: the site runs on Sitecore Cloud, which matches the posting's Sitecore preference directly.
  • Positioning: pro-first homepage ("On the job since 1865") with a Pro/DIY experience toggle, a top-level TDS/SDS hub, and four product categories. SDS/TDS access is a genuine strength, at parity or better versus competitors.
The Alex Plus contradiction. The Alex Plus product page promotes kitchen and bath, mold-free use, while its own FAQ answers "Can I use this in a shower? No," and DAP's Home Depot replies state it is not a kitchen and bath sealant. Shoppers get three different answers depending on where they look, and the reviews show the cost.
  • Missing on the product page as fetched: no reviews block and no buy or where-to-buy module, even though the Website Manager role owns ratings and reviews and where-to-buy. DAP reviews exist and syndicate out to Home Depot and Target labeled "from DAP.com"; they are just not shown on DAP's own pages.
  • Legacy residue: cms.dap.com is still indexed, including a Caulk + Sealant Selector that does not exist on the new site and a store locator that can return "No Sellers Found." The guided finder was effectively lost in the migration.
  • Discovery: category listings render through JavaScript ("Loading products..."), and localization today is Canada English/French only. No US Spanish site exists.

The competitor headline

GE Sealants (Henkel) is the biggest direct competitor: Henkel ranks first globally in caulk, DAP second, and GE claims the number one silicone brand position in the same categories and retailers. GE leads on a project-first homepage product finder, a homepage social feed, and fresh pro-depth how-tos. Gorilla, an adhesives-led brand used here as a digital benchmark, leads on a 24/7 AI assistant, quick decision guides, homepage email capture, and reviews visible from its own site. Neither competitor, nor DAP, offers a coverage calculator, live store inventory, an online warranty claim flow, a US Spanish site, or a pro loyalty program. The full side-by-side is on the comparison page.
